OFFLINE BYTE LOWER BOUNDS: PROOF AND SCOPE
Implementation status at creation: written only; not compiled or executed.

Definitions
M is memory capacity, A is the sum of all INSERT byte sizes, W and R_bw are
write/read bandwidths. After the final SWITCH every operation is PROBE and
partition sizes s_p are fixed. Index all suffix probes 1..n, including empty
targets. There is no assumed predecessor before suffix probe 1.

1. Disjoint windows and adjacent compulsory reads
Place cut i immediately AFTER service of probe i and BEFORE its periodic
callback, if any. Cut 0 is the suffix boundary. Window (j,i] includes the
periodic work of probe j, when j>0, and ends before periodic work of probe i.
Thus windows are disjoint; not charging the last periodic work is safe.

Let D(j+1..i) be the total bytes of DISTINCT partitions served in the window.
At its start at most M bytes are resident. Every other distinct required
partition must be loaded during the window, so reads >= D(j+1..i)-M.

When consecutive serviced targets p!=q satisfy s_p+s_q>M, q cannot be resident
at the service instant of p. It must be loaded before service of q, even if
the load happens in the periodic callback after p. Let F_i accumulate s_q for
these adjacent pairs through i, with F_0=F_1=0. The service intervals belonging
to different adjacent pairs do not overlap, so window reads >= F_i-F_j.
Consequently each window costs at least max(F_i-F_j, D(j+1..i)-M).

2. Strongest sum over window partitions
Let B_0=0. The maximum lower bound obtainable by disjoint windows is
 B_i = max_{0<=j<i} [B_j + max(F_i-F_j, D(j+1..i)-M)].
All window weights are nonnegative because F is nondecreasing. Put B_i=F_i+E_i.
Then E_0=0 and
 E_i = max(E_{i-1}, max_{0<=j<i}(E_j+F_j+D(j+1..i)-M)-F_i).
To see the first term, the F branch contributes max_j E_j=E_{i-1}; this follows
inductively from monotonicity of E.

Maintain G_j=E_j+F_j+D(j+1..i). Before processing probe i insert cut i-1 with
the OLD E_{i-1}+F_{i-1}. If its target previously appeared at L (L=0 if unseen),
only cuts j in [L,i-1] newly acquire its byte weight. A range-add/range-max lazy
segment tree implements the recurrence in O(n log n) time and O(n) memory.
All bytes, F/E/G values and DP operations are signed 64-bit integer exact.
Legal N<=5,000,000 and M<=4 GiB keep their magnitudes below 2^63.

3. Incompatible subsequences, independently
Take any subsequence of service positions whose neighboring selected targets
are different and have size sum >M. Between those two service instants the
later target must be loaded. The selected service intervals do not overlap;
sum the byte weights of all selected targets except the first.

val[q] is the best such subsequence ending at a previously seen q. Unseen
partitions have NEG, not zero. When q is served, its value becomes
 max(old_val[q], 0, s_q + max_seen_p!=q,s_p+s_q>M val[p]).
The zero term is the first REAL occurrence as an uncharged anchor. Query before
updating q. Leaves are ordered by (size,id); a range-max tree stores the best
two distinct owners, permitting strict size threshold and exclusion of q.
This costs O(n log Q) time and O(Q) memory. Empty partitions cannot be part of
an incompatible pair since every s_p<=M. Let L_path=max_q val[q].

The path and window arguments can charge the same reads. Therefore use
 L=max(B_n,L_path), NEVER B_n+L_path.

4. Couple reads and writes over the entire case
Let R_tot be actually executed LOAD bytes, S be SPILL bytes, Ap be disk append
INSERT bytes, and m_end be final resident bytes. Starting resident byte count
is zero, hence exactly
 m_end = A - Ap + R_tot - S.
Therefore total write bytes S+Ap=A+R_tot-m_end >= max(0,A+R_tot-M).
Executed reads satisfy R_tot>=L, so
 write_byte_LB=max(0,A+L-M),
 IO_LB=write_byte_LB/W + L/R_bw.
There is no final-flush assumption. This omits nonnegative miss penalties and
decision time, giving a lower bound on actual Score. Ignored SPILL0, duplicate
LOAD, or other ignored actions contribute no bytes and do not affect identity.
Final-empty partitions remain resident because official SPILL0 is ignored;
their probes contribute zero bytes but retain their positions and adjacency.

5. Relation to the older small-Q suffix relaxation
That relaxation permits a free feasible suffix resident set S_0 with prefix
write charge A-size(S_0). Its suffix transfers obey the same capacity/service
window/path conditions. If its suffix reads/spills are R_s/S_s, then
 A-size(S_0)+S_s=A+R_s-m_end.
Thus each of its feasible trajectories costs at least our raw IO bound, even
before its nonnegative miss penalties. Taking its minimum preserves ordering:
 new real-arithmetic raw IO LB <= old real-arithmetic suffix relaxation.
The verifier compares RAW values with numerical tolerance. The two tools have
different downward allowances, so their reported adjusted values need not be
strictly ordered without accounting for the old allowance.

6. Numerical and interpretation limits
The byte lower bounds are mathematical integer statements, not an exact oracle
for achievable scheduler cost. Only the final divisions use long double.
The displayed lower_bound subtracts .02 + 1e-12*max(1,raw) plus a small epsilon
term. This is a practical conservative adjustment, NOT machine certification.
Verification allows .02+1e-12*scale for saved SDK Score and old-bound checks.
The gap (C-LB)/C is only an UPPER BOUND on possible improvement. It is neither a
promise of attainable savings nor a measurement of offline-optimal regret.
No future information, true p/B, or offline result may enter online features.
